## Whole Summa (My class list)

Latin text of the complete Summa Theologiae (all four parts) from github.com/androur/Summa-Theologica-Latin (MIT licence; text from the Corpus Thomisticum and aquinas.cc), built into data/summa/ by tools/summa/build_summa.py, with the English of the Dominican Fathers' translation (Benziger 1947) from github.com/Jacob-Gray/summa.json (public domain dedication). Word forms are matched to the app's parsed corpora, then to forms generated by src/morph.js.

## Library

Latin texts from The Latin Library (via the CLTK collection), built into data/library/ by tools/library/build_library.py. The Old Testament in the Library is the Clementine Vulgate with the Douay-Rheims (Challoner), from bible_databases (`bd`), as for the New Testament.

## Canon law

The Decretals of Gregory IX and the Decretum Gelasianum from The Latin Library (public domain). The 1983 Code is not copied (© Libreria Editrice Vaticana; English © Canon Law Society of America): data/canon1983.json holds only each canon's page on vatican.va, taken from github.com/joconn819/codex-iuris-reader by tools/canon/build_canon_links.py. The 1917 Code (public domain) is in the Library: Latin text from the PDF kept in github.com/realitix/magisterium (A-pre-vatican-ii/droit-canonique; originally documentacatholicaomnia.eu), turned to text with pdftotext and divided by titulus in tools/library/build_extra.py.

## Lewis & Short

Lewis and Short, A Latin Dictionary (1879), from the Perseus Digital Library's XML (CC BY-SA 3.0) via github.com/IohannesArnold/lewis-short-json, built into data/ls/ by tools/data/build_ls.py. It opens under the word panel; for words the parsed texts never met, its headwords are matched to the word's ending as a labelled guess.

## Wiktionary word forms

Latin word forms from Wiktionary (CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0, via Wiktextract), as collected in James Penfold's Lexicon Scholasticum (github.com/piakobos/lexiconscholasticum), exported from its `forms` table and built into data/wkt/ by tools/data/build_wkt.py; only forms occurring in the app's texts are kept. Used for words the parsed texts never met, with all possible parsings shown.

## Wiktionary and Du Cange

From the Latin dictionary entries of Lexicon Scholasticum (exported as CSV), built into data/dict/ by tools/data/build_dicts.py: Wiktionary's English senses of Latin words (CC BY-SA; inflected-form entries left out) and Du Cange, Glossarium mediae et infimae Latinitatis (1883–87; digital edition of the École nationale des chartes). Gaffiot (French, partial) is not used.

## St Thomas's other works, manuals and readers

St Thomas's other works and Cajetan's commentary on the Summa, Latin with English where there is one, from github.com/antstratfordlol-bit/corpus-thomisticum (Corpus Thomisticum; Kenny's collection), by tools/library/build_opera.py. Zigliara's Summa philosophica, Mazzella's De Religione et Ecclesia and Boethius from the scans in Lexicon Scholasticum's reading-additions.json (OCR corrected by tools/library/ocrfix.py); Phaedrus, Eutropius, Lhomond and Erasmus from The Latin Library; by tools/library/build_readers.py. All added to the Library by tools/library/add_works.py. The online page reads these (and Du Cange) from the phone app's site.
