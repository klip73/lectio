## Whole Summa (My class list)

Latin text of the complete Summa Theologiae (all four parts) from github.com/androur/Summa-Theologica-Latin (MIT licence; text from the Corpus Thomisticum and aquinas.cc), built into data/summa/ by tools/summa/build_summa.py. Word forms are matched to the app's parsed corpora, then to forms generated by src/morph.js.
